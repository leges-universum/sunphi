    Payment protection

    Recipient spending lock

    Apply a 24-hour spending lock to an individual payment. The recipient receives the funds at settlement, but cannot move the locked amount until the delay ends.

    This option does not reverse a payment or protect other unlocked funds.

    Payment protection

    Send after 24 hours

    Hold an outgoing payment before execution, with time to cancel it.

    This is a separate option from the recipient spending lock.
