    Next: Your wallet

    Verification & independence

    Ten logical witnesses, seven required confirmations. This trial uses shared administration; logical nodes do not establish independent operators. Freshness is checked again before payment.

    Backup & recovery

    Save your encrypted key, receipts, invoices and saved payments. Keep the independent public network profile separately.

    Restore creates a new file; it cannot restore freshness or reverse a payment.

    How your wallet verifies state

    Zero-knowledge proofs, authenticated history, independent archive evidence and seven fresh witness confirmations are checked before spending.

    Automatic lock after five minutes of inactivity, on screen lock and when switching directions.

    Password & signing keys

    A backup can use a new password. This does not revoke older copies or change the network signing key. Global key replacement from the previous network is not available in this trial.

    Node rewards & controller changes

    Reward ownership registration and administrator controller replacement from the previous network do not apply to this trial. No reward income is promised.
